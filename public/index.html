Why Aminy

Built on trust

Genuine Parts Only

We never cut corners. Every repair uses manufacturer-grade or OEM-equivalent parts so your device performs like new.

Lifetime Warranty

Every repair comes with a limited lifetime warranty on parts and labour. Physical damage and liquid damage are not covered — but if our repair fails, we fix it free.

Same-Day Turnaround

Most repairs are done in 30 minutes to 2 hours. Walk in, get it fixed, walk out — usually the same day.

No Fix, No Fee

If we can't repair your device, you pay nothing. We believe in results, not just effort.
